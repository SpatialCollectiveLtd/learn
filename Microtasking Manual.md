## 🚀 Getting Started

### What You Need:
- **Smartphone** with internet connection
- **Valid phone number** starting with 07 (Safaricom, Airtel, or Telkom)
- **Registration** completed by your field trainer

### How to Access the App:
1. Open your phone browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.)
2. Go to: **micro.spatialcollective.co.ke**
3. Bookmark this page for easy access

---

## 🔑 How to Login

### Step 1: Enter Your Phone Number
1. On the login page, you'll see a phone number field
2. Enter your registered phone number in this format: **0712345678**
3. **Important:** Use only mobile numbers starting with 07
4. Landline numbers (starting with 01) are NOT allowed

### Step 2: Submit and Wait
1. Tap the **"Continue"** button
2. Wait for the green loading spinner
3. If successful, you'll see "Loading your tasks..."

### Common Login Issues:
- **"Phone number must be 10 digits starting with 07"**
  - ✅ Correct: 0712345678
  - ❌ Wrong: +254712345678, 712345678, 0112345678

- **"Phone number not registered"**
  - Contact your field trainer in your settlement
  - They will help register your number

- **"Too many login attempts"**
  - Wait a few minutes before trying again
  - Check your internet connection

## 📊 Understanding Your Dashboard

After login, you'll see your personal dashboard with:

### Today's Progress Section
- **Large number**: Tasks you've completed today
- **Progress bar**: Shows your daily completion (green = target reached)
- **Target**: Your daily goal is **300 tasks maximum**

### Statistics Cards
- **Campaigns Completed**: How many different campaigns you've finished today
- **Average Time**: How long you take per task (in seconds)

### Action Buttons
- **"Start Your Tasks"** or **"Continue Tasks"**: Begin working
- **"Refresh Stats"**: Update your progress numbers
- **"Logout"**: Sign out of the app

### What the Colors Mean:
- 🔴 **Red bar**: Still working towards your daily target
- 🟢 **Green bar**: Daily target reached (well done!)

## 🎯 Completing Tasks

### Step 1: Start Working
1. From your dashboard, tap **"Continue Tasks"**
2. Wait for the page to load
3. You'll see the task interface

### Step 2: Understanding the Task Page

#### Header Section:
- **Back arrow**: Return to dashboard
- **Your name**: Confirms you're logged in correctly
- **Logout**: Sign out when finished

#### Progress Section:
- **Progress bar**: Shows completion in current campaign
- **Numbers**: "X of Y" tasks completed in this campaign

#### Question Card:
- **Campaign Title**: Name of the current project
- **Question**: What you need to decide about each image
- **Image**: The picture you need to analyze
- **Answer Buttons**: "No" (red) and "Yes" (green)

### Step 3: Analyze the Image

**⚠️ IMPORTANT - Take Your Time!**
1. **Wait** for the image to fully load (loading spinner will disappear)
2. **Study** the image carefully
3. **Read** the question again if needed
4. **Think** about your answer - don't rush!
5. **Tap** either "No" or "Yes" based on the question

### Step 4: Submit Your Answer
1. Buttons will be **gray and disabled** while image loads
2. Once image loads, buttons turn **red** (No) and **green** (Yes)
3. Tap your chosen answer
4. Wait for the **spinning circle** to disappear
5. Next image will automatically appear

### What Happens Next:
- **New Image**: Another task from the same campaign
- **Campaign Complete**: You'll see a completion message
- **Daily Target Reached**: You've completed 300 tasks for today
- **No More Tasks**: All campaigns finished

## ✨ Quality Guidelines

### ⏰ Response Time Rules
- **Take your time** - there's no rush!
- **Minimum 2 seconds** per task (responses under 2 seconds are flagged)
- **Study each image** properly before answering
- **Quality over speed** - accuracy is more important than being fast

### 🔍 How to Give Good Answers
1. **Read the question carefully** at the top of the page
2. **Look at the entire image** - don't just glance
3. **Consider the context** of what you're seeing
4. **Be honest** in your assessment
5. **When in doubt**, look longer rather than guessing quickly

### 📱 Image Loading Issues
If an image won't load:
1. **Wait** - sometimes it takes time on slow internet
2. **Check** your internet connection
3. **Don't tap buttons** while loading (they're disabled for a reason)
4. **Refresh** the page if image fails completely

## 🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

### 🚫 Login Problems

**Issue: "Network error. Check your connection"**
- ✅ **Solution**: Check your internet/data connection
- Try switching between WiFi and mobile data
- Move to an area with better signal

**Issue: App won't load or is very slow**
- ✅ **Solution**: Close and reopen your browser
- Clear browser cache (ask someone to help if needed)
- Restart your phone if problems persist

### 📷 Image Problems

**Issue: Image not displaying**
- ✅ **Wait** 10-15 seconds for slow connections
- ✅ **Refresh** the page (pull down on the page)
- ✅ **Check** your data/WiFi connection

**Issue: Image is blurry or unclear**
- ✅ **Zoom in** by tapping the image (if your browser allows)
- ✅ **Rotate** your phone to landscape mode
- ✅ **Ensure** you're in good lighting to see your screen clearly

**Issue: Buttons are gray and won't work**
- ✅ This is **normal** - wait for image to finish loading
- Don't tap until buttons turn red and green

### 🔄 App Freezing or Hanging

**Issue: App stops responding**
- ✅ **Close** your browser completely
- ✅ **Reopen** browser and go back to the website
- ✅ **Login again** with your phone number

**Issue: Stuck on loading screen**
- ✅ **Wait** 30 seconds first
- ✅ **Refresh** the page
- ✅ **Check** your internet connection

### 📊 Dashboard Issues

**Issue: Progress not updating**
- ✅ **Tap** "Refresh Stats" button
- ✅ **Go back** to dashboard and check again
- ✅ **Logout and login** again if still not working
